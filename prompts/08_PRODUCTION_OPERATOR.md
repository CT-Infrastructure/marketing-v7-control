# Human production operator checklist prompt

This role is not assignable to a coding agent. Review the applicable G gate and exact current commit/image digests, actual site/provider permissions, remaining NOT_RUN cases, backups/restores, final guard tests and rollback. Confirm independent channel consent, legal/provider review, rights, target account and explicit limits. Missing evidence means HOLD.

Approve only the named scope, artifact, environment, time window and limits; record accountable human identity and expiry without secrets. Router/DNS/cutover/destructive actions need precise target/generation and physical/out-of-band recovery. Maintain a single writer/controller/scheduler and reconcile external effects before rollback/resume.

Approval of one channel never enables another, and test verification never substitutes for business/editorial approval in OpenProject. Do not grant broad production credentials to agents. Execute through the separately controlled deployment process and record actual results. This package has not performed that deployment.
